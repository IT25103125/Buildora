The Buildora Protocol

How We Protect You From Financial & Construction Risk

In traditional home building, the client bears all the risk. On Buildora, clients and architects brainstorm freely, while Buildora acts as the impartial guarantor, certified budget auditor, and milestone escrow holder.

01 Phase 1: Creative Dialogue

Direct Concept Discussions With No Strings Attached

You browse through verified architects, review their portfolio, and schedule free design concept sessions. You share your land boundaries, zoning rules, lifestyle wishes, and photos of styles you love.

What is strictly prohibited:

Architects on Buildora cannot demand private advance deposits, side retainers, or direct bank transfers. All discussions remain purely conceptual until Buildora audits the project.

02 Phase 2: Independent Audit

Buildora Translates Blueprints Into a Guaranteed Budget

When the architect drafts the schematic layout, it is routed into the Buildora Technical Desk. Our licensed Quantity Surveyors (QS) and structural specialists conduct a comprehensive structural and bill-of-quantities (BOQ) audit.

What we audit:

Soil and foundation requirements, structural steel, concrete grades, MEP (electrical & plumbing), window curtain wall engineering, and accurate market labor rates.

03 Phase 3: The Offer & Choice

You Review the Locked Budget — Deal Only If You Are Delighted

Buildora presents you with the final verified dossier. It contains an itemized cost projection, guaranteed completion timeline, and material standards.

The Buildora Promise:

If you like the budget and timeline, you approve it and Buildora formalizes the contract. If you do not approve, you walk away completely free of debt or legal commitments.

04 Phase 4: Milestone Escrow

Funds Stay Protected in Escrow, Released Only on Quality Pass

Your payments are deposited into a secure Buildora Escrow Trust Account. As construction reaches key milestones (Foundation, Superstructure, Roofing, Finishing), Buildora engineers inspect the site in person before a single rupee is released.

Zero Desertion Risk:

If a contractor or architect defaults, Buildora's reserve fund steps in and our vetted backup teams complete the milestone with zero extra charge to you.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Buildora strictly forbid direct payments to architects? +

Direct payments expose clients to immense vulnerabilities: architects might take substantial advances without delivering actionable engineering drawings, or they may design aesthetic structures that contractors quote 2x more to build. By inserting Buildora as the escrow intermediary and budget auditor, both parties are protected.

What if I don't like the budget Buildora calculates? +

You are under zero obligation. You can ask the architect to simplify the design (value engineering) to fit a smaller budget, or you can walk away without any penalty.

How does the architect get paid? +

Architects love Buildora because they are guaranteed payment! Once you approve the project budget, the architectural fees are locked into the milestone schedule. When the architect delivers schematic drawings, municipal approval drawings, and structural CAD packages, Buildora releases their funds automatically without client delays.

Who actually constructs the house? +

Buildora pairs the architect's designs with tier-1 chartered civil contractors vetted for financial stability, heavy machinery availability, and concrete quality certifications. Buildora oversees the contract execution until the day keys are handed over.
